Visible ellipsoid and rounded box brushes support color, metallic, smoothness and glow. Dye brushes add flat details to the underlying surface in recipe order. Version 3 recipes retain their original object-space interpretation. Invisible MudBun No Op brushes serve as pivots and parent the pieces they drive. Flecs stores ChildOf relationships and rest poses. MudBun stays active and renders from the moving brushes. The latest accepted recipe is saved locally. Startup leaves the photo object empty. The original photo stays separate from the recipe.
